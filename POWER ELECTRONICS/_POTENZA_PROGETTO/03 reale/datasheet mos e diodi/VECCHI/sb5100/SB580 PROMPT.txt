## 📄 SB580 — Key Datasheet Specs

From various sources:

* It is a Schottky barrier rectifier, 80 V, 5 A. ([onsemi][1])
* Maximum forward voltage: ~0.85 V at 5 A ([futurlec.com][2])
* Reverse current (I_R): up to ~0.5 mA at rated reverse voltage ([futurlec.com][2])
* Junction capacitance (C_j): ~380 pF @ 4 V reverse bias, 1 MHz measurement ([DigiKey][3])

These give you rough anchors for model parameters (saturation current, ideality, junction capacitance, series resistance, etc.).

## 🔍 Explanation & Tuning Suggestions

* `Is` (reverse saturation current) — for a Schottky, this may be relatively high compared to normal diode. Start with something like `1e-4 A` and adjust.
* `Rs` — series resistance to model the voltage drop at high current. Try something modest (e.g. 0.05 Ω) and tune.
* `N` (ideality / emission coefficient) — e.g. 1.1 to 2.0 depending on curve shape.
* `Bv`, `Ibv` — breakdown voltage and reverse current at breakdown. Use ~80 V for Bv. `Ibv` small leakage current.
* `Cjo` — junction capacitance (zero-bias). Use ~380 pF (datasheet) for Cjo. ([DigiKey][3])
* `M` — grading coefficient (between 0.3 and 0.5) for depletion capacitance.
* `Eg` — energy bandgap parameter (often ~0.7 eV) helps to model temperature / voltage dependence.

You will iterate: simulate forward I–V and reverse leakage in LTspice, compare curves to datasheet, adjust parameters until reasonably matching.
